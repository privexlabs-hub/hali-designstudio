03 · Colour

Warm neutrals, one accent, hues for meaning

Deep Sea is used for the brand mark and for committing or agreed states only. Blue, amber, red and green carry meaning and are never decoration. Contrast ratios are computed from the exact values. Dark values apply in the agency workspace and this studio only — public pages, client portals and emails stay light.

06 · Radius & shadow

Borders over shadows

Cards are 1px --border-default. Shadows are for overlays and floating previews only. The dialog scrim is the only transparency. No glass, no left-border accent cards.

07 · Motif

The line carries certainty

Dotted = suggested by Hali. Dashed = not yet agreed. Solid = recorded. Colour adds who must act. The motif works in greyscale and in print.

{{ s.meaning }}
Imagery and placeholders
Agency work image
Imagery comes from the agency's own work only — no stock, illustration or gradients. Empty slots use the diagonal hatch. Crop to fill; keep subjects clear of text. For text on photos, use a flat Ink overlay of 30–60% so text reaches 4.5:1.
Iconography — Lucide, 2px outline
Sizes 14 / 16 / 18 / 22px. Colour --icon-secondary unless it carries state. Status icons always sit beside text. Sparkle is reserved for Hali suggestions.
Charts and data
Illustrative · engagement value
{{ r.label }} {{ r.value }}
Never sum potential and approved values together. Use solid fills for recorded figures and dashed outlines for proposed ones. Label axes and values directly; avoid legends where a label fits. Always show currency and basis. Charts never animate.

09 · Components

Components and states

Live design-system components. Hover shifts one neutral step; press one further step; no scale or bounce. Focus is a 2px page-colour gap plus a 2px blue ring. Motion is 120–200ms on opacity and colour only, and nothing animates on records or money.

Navigation and cards
Cards are used only where a record is a unit (scope item, change order, decision). Lists are rows with dividers, not card grids. On phones, tables become stacked cards and dialogs become bottom sheets.
10 · Slides

Presentations and documents

16:9 at 1920×1080. Paper is the default slide background; Deep Sea marks section dividers; Ink for closing slides. Text never below 24px at full size. One idea per slide. Logo lockup top-left on title slides, symbol bottom-right elsewhere.
